● PEGASSI

Belgian electronic artist Pegassi, the creative alter ego of Sam Huybrecht, has emerged as one of Europe’s most exciting next-generation acts. His sound is a high-energy fusion of trance, techno and rave, rooted in the hip-hop he grew up with and elevated by a signature element that fans instantly recognize: the “bells.” What began as a sonic motif has evolved into a defining symbol of his artistry, shaping some of his biggest live moments and performances.

Pegassi’s rise accelerated with his breakout club anthem “Yoyoyo,” a track1 that set the tone for the hybrid, emotionally-charged rave identity he would continue perfecting. Follow-up singles like “Spectrals Bells” and the explosive “227kg” amplified his momentum; the latter became a true cult phenomenon, earning millions of streams and cementing the “bells”2 as an essential part of his sonic DNA.

His catalogue has grown into a global streaming presence, including “Bring It Back,” released on Berlin’s iconic Live From Earth label. On stage, Pegassi has established himself across Europe’s most respected festivals and clubs.3

His global footprint continues to expand through strong worldwide touring,4 with yearly runs across Europe, Australia, North America and South America that have earned him a devoted international following.
